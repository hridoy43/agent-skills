# Content format execution

Use after audience, message, evidence, stage, destination, and action are known. Every asset has one job and one action; key meaning, claims, disclosures, and accessibility live outside decorative imagery; title or cover, opening, body, CTA, and destination stay consistent; mark what is approved, what needs evidence, and what production must not imply.

| Format | Job | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short-form video | Earn and reward quick attention | Hook, one idea, proof or example, visual beats, captions, close |
| Promo or launch video | Explain value, build confidence | Promise, problem/progress, demo, proof, CTA, production brief |
| YouTube / long-form | Deliver depth | Title/thumbnail contract, opening, outline or script, evidence, recap, CTA |
| Thumbnail or cover | Make topic and stakes legible | Concept, subject, hierarchy, minimal text, small-size check, export specs |
| Carousel | Teach or persuade in sequence | Cover, panel jobs, proof, summary, CTA |
| Static social | Orient around one idea | Hierarchy, copy relationship, alt text, CTA |
| Text post or thread | Explain or invite discussion | Opening, useful body, evidence, close, links or CTA |

- **Video:** state what the viewer will learn, see, or do (no disconnected shock hook); demonstrate before repeating claims; write for voice and for muted viewing; specify platform, placement, duration, aspect ratio, safe areas, captions, audio, assets, presenter, pacing, and CTA timing when they matter; long-form fulfills the title and thumbnail promise early.
- **Thumbnail or cover:** one dominant subject, promise, tension, or outcome; complements rather than repeats the title; minimal high-contrast text legible at real size; brand recognizable without hurting clarity; no fabricated reactions, outcomes, interfaces, or authority.
- **Carousel, static, text:** one idea per panel (cover promises, middle delivers, last summarizes or invites); static creative makes sense without its caption; text posts carry substance, not bait; include alt text, captions, disclosures, and descriptive links.
- **Measure by job:** qualified reach, retained view, completion, save, share, useful reply, click, destination engagement, lead quality, assisted conversion, activation, revenue. Attention that hurts trust or customer quality is not success.
