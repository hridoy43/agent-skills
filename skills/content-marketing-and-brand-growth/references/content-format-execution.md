# Content format execution

Use this reference for platform and format adaptation after the audience, message, evidence, campaign stage, destination, and intended action are known.

## Shared quality contract

- Give each asset one primary job and one appropriate action.
- Match the opening to audience awareness and placement.
- Keep important meaning, claims, disclosures, and accessibility support available outside decorative imagery.
- Adapt the idea rather than duplicating copy across platforms.
- Preserve continuity between title or cover, opening, content, CTA, and destination.
- Specify what is approved, what needs evidence, and what production must not imply.

## Format map

| Format | Primary job | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short-form video | Earn and reward quick attention | Hook, one idea, proof/example, visual beats, captions, close |
| Promo or launch video | Explain value and build confidence | Promise, problem/progress, demonstration, proof, CTA, production brief |
| YouTube or long-form video | Deliver depth and sustain useful attention | Title/thumbnail contract, opening, section outline or script, evidence, recap, CTA |
| Thumbnail or cover | Make the topic and stakes legible | Concept, subject, hierarchy, minimal text, small-size check, export constraints |
| Carousel | Teach or persuade through sequence | Cover, panel jobs, proof/example, summary, CTA |
| Static social creative | Orient around one idea | Visual hierarchy, copy relationship, alt text, CTA |
| Text post or thread | Explain, discuss, or invite response | Opening, useful body, evidence, close, links or CTA |

## Video

- State what the viewer will learn, see, or be able to do; do not use a disconnected shock hook.
- Prefer product behavior, demonstration, attributable evidence, or a concrete example over repeated claims.
- Write for spoken clarity when voiceover is used and for silent comprehension when the placement commonly starts muted.
- Define platform, placement, duration, aspect ratio, safe areas, captions, audio, footage/assets, presenter or voice, pacing, and CTA timing when they affect production.
- For long-form content, make the title and thumbnail promise explicit in the opening and fulfill it before expanding into secondary material.

## Thumbnail and cover

- Use one dominant subject, promise, tension, comparison, or outcome.
- Complement the title instead of repeating it word for word when possible.
- Keep text minimal, high-contrast, and legible at actual display size.
- Preserve brand recognition without sacrificing topic clarity.
- Do not fabricate reactions, outcomes, interfaces, authority, or capabilities.

## Carousel, static, and text

- Assign one idea or transition to each carousel panel; the cover promises, the middle delivers, and the final panel summarizes or invites the next step.
- Keep static creative understandable without relying on the caption for its only context, while avoiding duplicate paragraphs inside the image.
- Use text posts for substance, conversation, or clear explanation rather than empty engagement bait.
- Include alt text, captions, disclosures, and descriptive links as the platform allows.

## Measurement and learning

Choose metrics by asset job: qualified reach, retained view, completion, save, share, useful reply, click, destination engagement, lead quality, assisted conversion, activation, or revenue. Define a review window and record what to repeat, change, or stop. Do not call attention successful when it degrades trust, destination performance, or customer quality.
