# Behavior and decision design

Use when message order alone can't explain the behavior. These are optional diagnostics, not story templates; pick one only if it changes execution.

| Model | Fit | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elaboration Likelihood | High-consideration, risky, technical, regulated | How deeply will they scrutinize arguments and evidence? |
| Fogg (B=MAP) | One discrete action: click, submit, buy, permit | Are motivation, ability, and a timely prompt present together? |
| COM-B | Onboarding, adoption, retention, continuing behavior | Do capability, opportunity, and motivation support it? |
| EAST | Generating interventions | Can it be easier, more attractive, social, timely—without manipulation? |
| Decision-friction audit (LIFT-style) | Existing page, flow, form, checkout | Are value, relevance, clarity, distraction, anxiety, and timing handled? |

Don't use ELM to justify weak arguments, Fogg for wide continuing behavior, COM-B for a lone CTA, or EAST as a nudge checklist; fix the offer before auditing friction.

## Depth of persuasion

- High elaboration: specific claims, mechanism, comparison, limits, proof provenance, terms, risk controls—weak claims backfire.
- Low elaboration: fast relevance, value, credibility, and next step—never hidden terms or unsupported cues.
- Mixed: immediate summary plus progressive detail without hiding decision-critical facts.

## Diagnose

Discrete action: motivation (valuable now?), ability (clear, affordable, accessible, low effort?), prompt (visible, timely, specific?). Continuing behavior: capability (knowledge, skill, confidence), opportunity (environment, timing, device, permissions, resources), motivation (goals and feelings). Match the fix to the cause: no emotional copy for an ability problem, no louder CTA for a capability gap, no urgency for weak product value.

## Friction audit

Check value and relevance, clarity of offer and consequences, unnecessary fields or steps or choices, anxiety from missing proof, privacy, delivery, cancellation, or recovery info, distractions, real and useful timing, accessibility and vulnerability. Remove accidental friction while preserving informed choice; never hide material facts, obstruct alternatives, preselect harmful defaults, or exploit vulnerability.
