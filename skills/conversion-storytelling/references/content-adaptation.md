# Content and media adaptation

For turning an approved product story into video, thumbnail, social, or campaign assets. Keep audience, promise, proof, tone, and destination; change only the expression. Campaign planning and publishing belong to `content-marketing-and-brand-growth`; production to the relevant media skill.

Brief only what changes the result: platform and placement, organic or paid, journey stage, format, size or duration, language, accessibility, viewer awareness and context, next action, available footage, screenshots, presenter, voice, music, brand assets, and the link to title, caption, destination, or sequence.

| Surface | Job | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promo video | Show a valuable change and credible mechanism | Hook, promise, problem/progress, demo or proof, CTA, duration |
| YouTube / long-form | Sustain attention and deliver promised depth | Title/thumbnail promise, opening contract, sections, evidence, recap, CTA |
| Short-form | One useful idea fast | Immediate context, one claim, visual proof, concise close |
| Thumbnail | Topic and stakes legible small | One dominant subject, hierarchy, minimal text, honest curiosity |
| Static social | Stop and orient | One idea, readable hierarchy, contrast, caption relationship |
| Carousel | Teach or persuade in sequence | Cover promise, one step per panel, proof, summary, next action |

- Video: match the opening to awareness (no unrelated shock hooks); say what the viewer will learn, see, or do; demonstrate before repeating claims; write for spoken clarity and muted viewing; captions, safe areas, pacing, and reduced-motion alternatives are requirements; CTA after enough value and proof; title, thumbnail, opening, CTA, and destination stay consistent.
- Thumbnail and static: one promise or tension, the real product, result, or person, short text distinct from the title, brand present but not a logo wall; never fabricate reactions, interfaces, results, authority, or urgency.
- Social: repurpose the idea, not the copy; one idea and one action per asset; disclosures, alt text, captions, and key meaning outside the image; define each asset's job in a sequence.
- Measure by surface: qualified views, completion, retention point, saves, shares, clicks, destination engagement, assisted conversion; never trade trust or lead quality for attention.
