# Production foundations

A checklist, not a mandate to add every subsystem.

- **Config:** central product/brand config; env validated at startup; local/test/preview/prod behavior without scattered `NODE_ENV` checks; committed `.env.example` without secrets; secret ownership, rotation, and CI injection; uncertain business values configurable.
- **Identity:** provider, session model, recovery, verification, tenancy, roles, service and support accounts, audit. Authorize every resource operation and scope queries by tenant or owner; negative tests for horizontal and vertical escalation.
- **Persistence:** source of truth, schema ownership, migrations, seeds, indexes, retention, export/deletion, backups and restore drills, residency, encryption. Expand/contract migrations; never pair irreversible data destruction with an ordinary deploy.
- **Integrations** (payments, email, SMS/push, files, search, maps, CMS, AI, CRM): owner, data sent, privacy basis, credential scope, adapter and error model, idempotency, retries, rate limits, timeouts, webhook signatures, dedupe, ordering, replay, sandbox, outage fallback, replacement path. No integration before its journey and failure behavior are defined.
- **Jobs and realtime:** request/response first; a durable queue only for work that must outlive requests, retry independently, or be scheduled (define idempotency, visibility timeout, dead-letter, concurrency, ordering, operator recovery). Realtime only when freshness needs push (reconnect, missed events, authz, presence, polling fallback).
- **Content:** typed files or MDX for developer-owned content; a CMS only for editorial workflow, permissions, preview, localization, or independent publishing. Never publish placeholders, drafts, unapproved translations, or invented claims.
- **Observability:** structured logs, correlation IDs from the transport boundary end to end, error monitoring, metrics, traces, scrubbing, sampling, dashboards, alert owners, incident response. The shared layer owns IDs, sampling, and transport; each feature owns its events, metric names, and alert owner. Product analytics stay separate from operational telemetry.
- **Delivery:** small backward-compatible releases; feature flags only for staged rollout, kill switches, experiments, or cross-deploy work, each with an owner and removal condition; risky releases define canary, health checks, migration order, rollback artifact, and owner.
- **CI and docs:** CI runs format, lint, typecheck, tests, build, dependency/security checks, and schema/content/locale validation; protect the default branch. Document setup, env and integrations, ADRs, content and translation editing, migrations and rollback, deploy and DNS, monitoring, and launch checklist.
- **Legal truth:** keep entity, address, registration, certifications, compliance, testimonials, metrics, partners, and claims configurable until verified; privacy and terms copy must match real data flows and get review.
